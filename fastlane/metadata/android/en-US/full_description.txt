Bruno's Meditation Timer is a simple, offline meditation and pranayama timer.

Features:

* Meditation presets with optional starting, intermediate, and ending bells.
* Infinite and timed meditation sessions.
* Pranayama presets with smooth visual guidance and generated breath tones.
* Session logging, CSV import/export, streak repair, and basic statistics.
* Local preset import/export.
* No account, ads, analytics, tracking, or network service is required for core use.

The app was built for personal meditation practice: quiet defaults, minimal
visual interruption during sessions, and enough structure to keep track of
practice over time.
